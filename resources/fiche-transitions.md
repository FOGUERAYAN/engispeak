# Fiche mémo : les mots de transition (Semaine 1, Session 2)

**Annoncer la structure** : First of all… / To begin with… / Let me walk you through three ideas.
**Enchaîner** : Then… / Next… / Building on that… / Which brings me to…
**Opposer** : However… / On the other hand… / That said…
**Conséquence** : As a result… / So… / That's why…
**Conclure** : To wrap up… / The one thing to remember is…

Exercice : résume un TED Talk en 60–90 secondes avec au moins 3 transitions.
